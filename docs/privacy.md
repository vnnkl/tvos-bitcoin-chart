# Privacy Policy — Bitcoin Terminal

_Last updated: July 3, 2026_

Bitcoin Terminal is a tvOS app that displays public cryptocurrency market data.

## Data we collect

**None.** Bitcoin Terminal does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Network requests

The app fetches publicly available market data directly from:

- **Binance** (`api.binance.com`, `stream.binance.com`) — Bitcoin price, candlestick, order book, and trade data
- **strc.live** — public SEC filing data related to the STRC ticker

These requests contain no personal information beyond what any HTTPS connection technically requires (your IP address, as seen by those services). Their handling of connection data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Local storage

App preferences (such as the selected timeframe) are stored only on your Apple TV using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` mechanism. They never leave the device.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
